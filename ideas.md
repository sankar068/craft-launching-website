# CRAFT — Launching Soon Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Nocturne Signal
Very dark, cinematic, and exacting: a quiet launch room with precise white typography, restrained ultraviolet light, and subtle technical markers.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Printed Future
Editorial and tactile: oversized black-and-cream type, paper-like grain, and a confident art-book composition that frames the launch as a cultural moment.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Infrared Bloom
A sharp, high-contrast studio world where warm coral light cuts through charcoal, creating a more expressive and human premium-tech tone.

**Probability:** 0.03

## Selected direction: Nocturne Signal

### Design Movement
Swiss International Typographic Style fused with cinematic digital art direction and luxury technology branding.

### Core Principles
1. Make typography the product: CRAFT should feel like a wordmark, not a heading.
2. Use silence as structure: generous negative space, precise alignment, and very little copy.
3. Treat motion as a signal: slow ambient movement, crisp state changes, no spectacle for its own sake.
4. Let one accent carry the energy: a restrained ultraviolet-blue highlight only where attention should land.

### Color Philosophy
The near-black canvas creates a private, gallery-like atmosphere. Soft white type provides clarity and restraint. A single ultraviolet signal suggests the launch is powered, alive, and approaching without turning the page into a neon interface.

### Layout Paradigm
An asymmetric poster-like composition: a slim header establishes the status, the hero wordmark sits slightly left of the visual center, the countdown occupies a lower horizontal band, and contact details anchor the bottom edge. Avoid centered-template symmetry while preserving a calm, intentional rhythm.

### Signature Elements
- A thin vertical signal line that connects header, hero, and countdown.
- Oversized CRAFT wordmark with a low-opacity ghost echo offset behind it.
- Compact numeric countdown set in a technical mono face, separated by hairline rules.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like touching a physical instrument: links brighten and shift a few pixels, the cursor creates a very soft local light response, and the countdown updates with an understated numeric crossfade.

### Animation
Use opacity and transform only. Reveal the header, eyebrow, wordmark, and countdown in a restrained stagger. Keep ambient orb drift slow and nearly subliminal. Pulse the launch-status dot once every two seconds. Respect reduced motion by disabling non-essential movement.

### Typography System
Display: Space Grotesk with tight tracking for the CRAFT wordmark. Utility/body: Manrope for readable microcopy. Countdown: IBM Plex Mono for precise numeric rhythm. Hierarchy is built with scale, tracking, and opacity rather than many font weights.

### Brand Essence
CRAFT is a digital studio for people who want high-quality websites and digital experiences with a point of view, not a template. Personality: exacting, curious, quietly bold.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are declarative and spare. CTAs are invitations, never hype. Microcopy sounds observant and confident.

Example lines:
- “A new digital standard is arriving.”
- “The signal is almost clear.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a custom-feeling all-caps wordmark with wide tracking and a clipped signal notch motif in the letter A, supported by a small geometric spark mark used as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
Ultraviolet Signal — #8B7CFF. It appears sparingly in the pulse, focus halo, and launch-state reveal.
